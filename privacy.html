InvestMAX 隐私政策

更新于 2026 年 11 月 3 日 · Last updated November 3, 2026
简体中文

数据存在哪里

你的持仓、交易记录和设置只保存在你的设备上。InvestMAX 没有账号系统，也没有自己的服务器，开发者看不到你的任何数据。

iCloud 同步（可选）

开启“iCloud 同步”后，数据保存在你自己的 iCloud 私有数据库中，由 Apple 提供加密与存储，只有登录同一 Apple ID 的设备能读取，开发者无法访问。

行情数据

为了显示价格，App 会向 Yahoo Finance、CoinGecko、Frankfurter 请求公开行情。请求里只有代码（例如 AAPL），不含你的数量、成本或身份信息。

AI 分析

只有你主动点击“开始分析”或使用截图识别时，持仓结构（名称、占比、收益率）或截图才会发送到你在设置里选择的 AI 服务商，并受该服务商隐私政策约束。未配置 API Key 时全部在本机计算。

密钥

AI 与交易所的 API Key 保存在系统钥匙串中，只用于你发起的请求。交易所只需要只读权限，InvestMAX 不能下单或提币。

购买

订阅与购买由 Apple 处理，InvestMAX 只从 App Store 获取“是否已购买”的状态，拿不到你的付款信息。

通知与小组件

价格提醒是在你的设备上生成的本地通知；小组件读取的是 App 写在本机共享容器里的数据，都不会上传。

统计与广告

InvestMAX 不含广告，不使用任何第三方统计或追踪 SDK，不收集设备标识符。

删除数据

在“我的 › 数据”中可以清空全部持仓；删除 App 会同时删除本机所有数据。开启过 iCloud 同步的，可在系统“设置 › Apple ID › iCloud › 管理储存空间”中删除 InvestMAX 的数据。

English

Where your data lives

Your holdings, trades and settings are stored only on your device. InvestMAX has no accounts and no servers of its own; the developer cannot see any of your data.

iCloud sync (optional)

If you turn on iCloud Sync, data is stored in your own private iCloud database, encrypted and stored by Apple and readable only by devices signed in to your Apple ID. The developer has no access.

Market data

To show prices, the app requests public quotes from Yahoo Finance, CoinGecko and Frankfurter. Requests contain only tickers (e.g. AAPL), never your quantities, costs or identity.

AI analysis

Only when you tap “Analyze” or use screenshot import is your portfolio structure (names, weights, returns) or the screenshot sent to the AI provider you chose in Settings, subject to that provider’s privacy policy. Without an API key, everything is computed on device.

Keys

AI and exchange API keys are stored in the system Keychain and used only for requests you start. Exchange keys need read-only permission; InvestMAX cannot trade or withdraw.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are handled by Apple. InvestMAX only receives whether you have purchased, never your payment details.

Notifications and widgets

Price alerts are local notifications created on your device; widgets read data the app writes to an on-device shared container. Nothing is uploaded.

Analytics and ads

InvestMAX has no ads, uses no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs, and collects no device identifiers.

Deleting data

Use Me › Data to clear all holdings; deleting the app removes all local data. If you used iCloud Sync, remove InvestMAX data in Settings › Apple ID › iCloud › Manage Storage.
